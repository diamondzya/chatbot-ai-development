Before you jump in

A few things to know.

Is this an official STI service?

No. STI Assist AI is an unofficial student prototype, not affiliated with or endorsed by STI. It uses the STI High School Student Handbook (July 2026). Official announcements and your school office take priority.

Do I need to create an account?

No sign-in is needed. On your first visit to the chat, you’ll be asked to read and accept the Terms of use. Your acceptance is remembered in this browser.

Can I rely on every answer?

Answers can be incomplete or incorrect, even when a page is cited. Use the source cards to verify handbook rules, and check important matters with your adviser or school office.

What happens to my conversation?

The conversation is kept in this tab’s memory and is lost when you refresh or leave the chat. Your questions go to the app’s server and, in AI mode, the configured AI provider. Unanswered questions may be logged with common personal details redacted to improve handbook coverage. Avoid sharing sensitive information. The chat’s About & privacy panel has more details.
